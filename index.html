Frequently Asked Questions

1️⃣ How do I convert a blog post to social media posts?

Simply paste your blog post into ContentSplitter, select your target platforms (Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram), and click Convert. Our AI will automatically optimize your content for each platform in 30 seconds.

2️⃣ Can I convert blog posts to Twitter threads?

Yes! ContentSplitter converts long-form blog posts into engaging Twitter threads, automatically breaking content into tweet-sized chunks (under 280 characters) while maintaining flow and readability.

3️⃣ Is ContentSplitter free to use?

Yes! We offer 3 free conversions per day with no credit card required. Premium users ($9.99/month) get unlimited conversions plus advanced features like multiple tones and multi-language support.

4️⃣ What platforms does ContentSplitter support?

ContentSplitter optimizes content for Twitter/X threads, LinkedIn posts, and Instagram captions. Each platform gets custom formatting, appropriate tone, and platform-specific best practices.

5️⃣ How does AI content repurposing work?

ContentSplitter uses advanced AI (Claude) to understand your content's key messages and rewrite them for each social media platform. It adjusts tone, length, formatting, and adds platform-specific elements like hashtags for Instagram or professional language for LinkedIn.

6️⃣ Can I edit the generated social media posts?

Yes! After conversion, you can review and edit all generated posts before copying them. Each post has a copy button for easy use.
